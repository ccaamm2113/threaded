              How the call works

              • One call per window. Each 15-minute round gets at most one call: BET UP, BET DOWN, or SIT OUT. A call fires only after the entry gates pass on 2 consecutive polls, then it latches — it stays until a sell trigger fires or the window rolls. No flip-flopping.
              • The probability is a pure contract blend. P(BTC finishes above the target) = 85% empirical history (how often similar moves cleared the target) + 15% parametric (zero-drift normal model from recent 1-minute volatility). No ML model, no timeframe vote, no second engine — the directional votes that used to poison the blend are gone.
              • Edge is net of the Kalshi taker fee. Net edge = blended probability × 100 − ask − fee. A call needs net edge above the threshold (3¢, tighter near expiry, +2¢ when choppy), probability beyond the bar, momentum agreeing, spread ≤ 10¢, ≥ 2 min left, and BTC at least 0.05% from the target. Near the target the signal is weakest, so the card says so explicitly: too close to call, no bet.
              • Sell triggers. Under 60s left (settlement lottery), ask ≥ 93¢ (bank it), net edge below zero on 2 polls, or the signal flipping with confirmation. A sold call stays sold until the window rolls.
              • Every sealed call is graded in public. The public record logs each call's seal price, outcome, and win/loss — verifiable against Kalshi's own settlement record. The record starts at v26; no win rate is claimed until the record earns one.
              • Kalshi's KXBTC15M settles on a 60-second CF Benchmarks index average — spot candles can differ near the target.
              • Compare the estimate to the executable Kalshi price and account for fees/spread/slippage. No trades are placed here.
              Inputs and limitations

              Inputs: Coinbase 1-minute candles (Kraken fallback), Kalshi KXBTC15M quotes + order-book depth, Coinbase order-book/trade flow, Hyperliquid perp funding + open interest, Fear & Greed sentiment, and an optional AI read (display-only — it never changes the call).

              Limitations: public feeds can lag; spot ≠ settlement index; the market is usually right, so genuine edge is rare and the card will often say SIT OUT — that is the honest answer, not a failure. Fees/slippage are modeled at the Kalshi schedule but fills vary. Not a validated profitable strategy — a contract can lose its full cost.
