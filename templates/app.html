          How the estimate works

          • Direction check scores conviction = 50% probability + 25% momentum + 25% edge vs the Kalshi ask. The model/history blend shifts with time left — history earns up to 75% weight near expiry, since the model is trained for a 15-minute horizon.
          • 🚀 STRONG LEAN (conviction ≥ 0.55), 🟢🔴 LEAN (≥ 0.28), 🟡 WAIT otherwise. Choppy markets (5-min range > 2× normal) demand 0.45 conviction; model/history disagreement caps at LEAN; negative edge is always a veto.
          • Buy timing needs 6 green checks including confirmation on 2 consecutive polls, a tight spread (≤ 10¢) and ≥ 2 min left. Sell timing watches for edge evaporation, trailing peak-edge fade, momentum flips and the sub-60s settlement lottery.
          • Kalshi's KXBTC15M settles on a 60-second CF Benchmarks index average — spot candles can differ near the target.
          • Compare the estimate to the executable Kalshi price and account for fees/spread/slippage. No trades are placed here.
          Model features and limitations

          Features: 1/3/5/15-min returns, rolling volatility, volume change & z-score, SMA distances, 5-min range, RSI(7/14), MACD + signal + histogram, Bollinger %B, ATR ratio, EMA-12/26 distance, 3v5 acceleration, plus ETH/SPY/QQQ 5-min returns when available.

          Model: L2 logistic regression (C=0.8, balanced) with sigmoid calibration (3-fold), trained on recent 1-min candles. Chronological holdout for sanity metrics.

          Limitations: public feeds can lag; spot ≠ settlement index; overlapping labels and regime shifts distort backtests; fees/slippage not fully modeled. Not a validated profitable strategy — a contract can lose its full cost.
